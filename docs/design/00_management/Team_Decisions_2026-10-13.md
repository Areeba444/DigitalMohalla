| D18 | Meaning of S1 "reciprocal rating clustering" (Algorithm 3, Overview §5.6) | Proposed during C1 with AI assistance (see `docs/ai-usage-log.md`), from measurements on the synthetic tiers and the literature; confirmed by Student 2 (Areeba Javed) on 14 October. The written formula r = (people the subject rated who rated them back) ÷ (people the subject rated) flags ordinary members, because after every loan both sides review each other: on the standard tier 6 of 8 ordinary members reached S1 ≥ 0.5, and ES-05's borrower scored like the ES-04 ring. Two-sided reviews are reciprocal by nature (Bolton, Greiner and Ockenfels 2013; Fradkin, Grewal and Holtz 2021), while collusion shows as groups that rate one another (Mukherjee, Liu and Glance 2012; Hooi et al. 2016). S1 is therefore the share of pairs among the subject's review partners (at least 3, 90-day window) who reviewed each other both ways, scaled as before, (r − 0.3) / 0.6. Measured: ES-04 ring 1.00, C3 family 1.00 (capped at level 3 by S4, as PU-02 intends), ES-05 0.00, 0 of 9 ordinary members at ≥ 0.5. Known limit: a ring that also trades with outsiders lowers its own density ("camouflage", Hooi et al. 2016) | Engine config 1.3.0-iteration1-draft (`s1_min_partners`); Algorithm doc §5; `feature/manipulation-checks` |
| D19 | What a suspected-retaliation flag changes (FR-T-09, ES-05) | Proposed during C1 with AI assistance; confirmed by Student 2 (Areeba Javed) on 14 October. Reviews do not enter the trust estimate or the trust edges (both use verified loan outcomes), so a retaliatory review cannot lower a borrower's trust or remove a trust connection. A low review (lowest rating ≤ 2) posted within 14 days after the subject's complaint about its author (a dispute they opened, or a low review of their own), about a loan whose return was confirmed, is set aside: left out of S1 and S4, recorded with the assessment's signals, and explained to the lender in neutral words. The engine writes nothing to the review table; confirming the flag is a moderator's decision (C2), so no one is accused or restricted automatically | Engine config 1.3.0-iteration1-draft (`retaliation_check`); Algorithm doc §5; `feature/manipulation-checks` |
