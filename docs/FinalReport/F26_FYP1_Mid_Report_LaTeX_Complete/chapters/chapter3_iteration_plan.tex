\chapter{Proposal Feedback and Iteration Plan}

\section{Proposal Feedback Summary}
During the FYP-1 Proposal Defense, the evaluation panel provided critical feedback:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Strengthen the Technical Core:} The panel noted that a standard CRUD application for community resource sharing was insufficient for an engineering capstone. The team was directed to elevate the computational trust engine, formalize the mathematical models, and substantiate the complex computing challenge.
    \item \textbf{Privacy-Preserving Locality:} While locality was recognized as vital for physical exchanges, the panel cautioned against collecting invasive location traces. The team was instructed to decouple eligibility verification from raw GPS coordinate storage.
    \item \textbf{Rigorous Software Engineering Lifecycle:} The project required full traceability, formal state modeling, robust concurrency controls, and automated verification rather than ad-hoc prototype development.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Iteration 1 Plan and Milestones}
The project execution was organized into an iterative sprint model divided into two major phases:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Part A: Design Baseline Integration (11 October 2026):} Harmonization of all structural, behavioural, and data models across the three team members into a single coherent baseline in \texttt{docs/design/}.
    \item \textbf{Part B: Vertical Slices Implementation (12--13 October 2026):}
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textbf{Slice A (Core Lifecycle):} Request-to-return domain aggregate, transition table, append-only PostgreSQL event store, FastAPI routes, and initial Expo web/mobile interface.
        \item \textbf{Slice B (Assurance Engine):} Outcome extraction (D11), Beta posterior estimation with similarity weighting (D17), trust-edge projection (ADR-0006), bounded $k$-hop search (Algorithm 2), decision policy (Algorithm 4), synthetic scenario suites, and lender evidence screens.
        \item \textbf{Slice C (Moderation \& Anti-Collusion):} Collusive burst detection, retaliatory rating mitigation, dispute filing, and moderator adjudication workflows.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\section{Work Completed for Iteration 1 vs.\ Future FYP Roadmap}
With Iteration 1 (Mid-Assessment Scope) 100\% implemented, verified, and tested across Slices A, B, and C, the future roadmap for FYP-2 encompasses:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Automated Background Schedulers:} Implementing asynchronous scheduled jobs (expiry, no-show detection, automated reminders, and overdue disputes: T15, T19, T24).
    \item \textbf{Secure Photo Uploads:} Adding camera/file attachment integration with client-side EXIF GPS metadata stripping (deferred from Iteration 1 per architectural decision D20).
    \item \textbf{Resident Pilot Beta Testing:} Conducting field usability trials and user experience benchmarks (NFR-U-02 to NFR-U-04) within real neighborhood pilot groups.
    \item \textbf{Extended Social Features:} Introducing counter-offer negotiations, loan period extensions, and secure intra-community direct messaging.
\end{enumerate}
