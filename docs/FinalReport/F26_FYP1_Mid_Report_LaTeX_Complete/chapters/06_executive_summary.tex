\section*{Problem}
Neighbourhood resource sharing unlocks substantial socio-economic and environmental value (SDG 11, SDG 12), but physical face-to-face handover introduces real physical, financial, and interpersonal risk. Conventional marketplace and listings platforms fail in this setting:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item New borrowers frequently possess zero direct interaction history (cold-start problem).
    \item Useful evidence often exists only indirectly through mutual community acquaintances.
    \item Ratings are vulnerable to reciprocal bias, retaliatory reviews, and coordinated collusion.
    \item Exposing exact GPS locations or personal identity data introduces severe privacy and harassment risks.
    \item Commercial platforms collapse uncertainty into a single opaque rating, penalizing newcomers.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Proposed Solution}
Digital Mohalla bridges this gap through a principled multi-layered architecture:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Privacy-Preserving Locality Verification:} Coarse, privacy-safe locality boundaries without storing GPS traces or CNIC data.
    \item \textbf{Event-Sourced Borrowing Lifecycle:} Append-only, cryptographically hash-chained event logs in PostgreSQL 16 guaranteeing tamper evidence and state replayability.
    \item \textbf{Evidence-Based Assurance Engine:} Bayesian recency-weighted Beta estimation with 90\% credible intervals, contextual similarity weighting, and bounded $k$-hop indirect trust propagation.
    \item \textbf{Transparent Safeguards and Human Moderation:} Decision rules (R1--R9) that separate evidence volume from transaction risk, recommending specific safeguards (e.g., daylight handover, identity inspection) or human moderator escalation rather than punitive automated bans.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Tiered Presentation Client:} A mobile and web application (built on React Native and Expo) presenting evidence layers, credible intervals, and community paths separately from lifecycle state.
\end{enumerate}
